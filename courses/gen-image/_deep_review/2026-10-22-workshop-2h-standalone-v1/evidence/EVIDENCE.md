## Existing decisions

1. The fictional campaign is 「島嶼果日｜炙香鳳梨茶果禮盒」.
2. The learner deliverables are one 1080×1350 social poster, one 1080×1080 extension, a reusable six-part prompt, and a completion check.
3. The first 20 minutes teach template and wireframe reading.
4. Six visual structures are taught by their standalone names: Bento Grid, Comparison, Step by Step, Founder Portrait, Sticker Set, and Lookbook Grid.
5. AI-generated copy is not treated as final text; formal Traditional Chinese copy is rebuilt and checked in Canva.
6. The current Deep Review is read-only. Findings do not authorize repairs.
